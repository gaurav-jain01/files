Call for Papers

We invite original, unpublished research across communication systems, intelligent computing, and applied technology. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review.

Topics

Suggested tracks

  • Track 01: Intelligent Systems: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Track 02: Data-Driven Discovery: Big Data, Analytics & Knowledge Engineering
  • Track 03: Intelligent Connectivity: IoT, Edge, and Cloud
  • Track 04: Trust & Security: Cybersecurity, Privacy & Blockchain
  • Track 05: Human-Centered Design: Immersive & Interactive Technologies
  • Track 06: Responsible Innovation: Computing for Society & Sustainability
  • Track 07: Smart Automation: Robotics, Autonomous Systems & Industry 5.0

Topics outside this list are welcome where they fit the conference scope — describe the fit briefly in your submission note.

Guidelines

Submission requirements

Length

Maximum 8 pages

If exceeds, ₹2500/- to be charged up to 3 extra pages. Maximum 6 authors allowed.

Originality

Unpublished work only

Submissions must not be under review elsewhere at the time of submission.

Review

Double-blind

Remove author names and affiliations from the manuscript before upload.

Posters

A1 size (23x33 inch)

Posters must be prepared on A1 size paper.
