Why attend

A focused venue for applied and theoretical work

Three tracks run across the programme, each reviewed by an independent panel drawn from the technical committee.

01

Communication Systems

Wireless networks, signal processing, 5G/6G architectures and next-generation protocols.

02

Intelligent Computing

Machine learning, pattern recognition and applied AI across domains and data types.

03

Systems & Security

Distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, IoT and cybersecurity research.

Publication

Peer-reviewed proceedings

Accepted papers are published in an indexed proceedings series and made available to the wider research community after the event.

Scope

Double-blind review

Every submission is assessed by at least two independent reviewers.

Format

Hybrid presentation

Present in person or join remotely — both formats receive equal standing.

Reach

Indexed proceedings

Full papers are archived and made citable after the conference closes.
